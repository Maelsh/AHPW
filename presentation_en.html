Precise scientific definitions to prevent semantic confusion

The S-Pendulum

Two identical, inverted hydraulic chambers placed diametrically opposite each other, connected by an S-shaped flow tube. Referred to as the **S-Pendulum**.

Container & Vessel

A specialized hydraulic cylinder where a traditional rod is replaced by a sliding solid heavy mass. Contains a flexible accordion bellows vessel that prevents any fluid leakage during compression.

Piston Weight

The heavy metallic block inside the container. It harnesses gravity to act as a double-acting mechanical actuator, squeezing or expanding the bladder to displace fluid.

Mother Wheel

The large outer structural wheel, suspended in the air on heavy-duty, low-friction ball bearings. It structurally houses all the nested pendulum wheels.

Coordinate & Angle System

0°/360° represents the absolute vertical top apex, 180° is the absolute bottom close to the ground, 90° is far-right, and 270° is far-left. Each pendulum spans diametric angles (e.g. 45° and 225°).

Pendulum Wheel

The individual inner circular structural frame that locks a single S-pendulum (two cylinders and the connecting S-tube) in place, nesting into the main Mother Wheel assembly.
